# 10 Must-Know Premiere Pro Shortcuts to Edit Faster

Editing in **Adobe Premiere Pro** can be much faster and more efficient when you use keyboard shortcuts.  
Instead of spending hours clicking through menus, these 10 essential shortcuts will help you work like a pro.  

---

## 1. Razor Tool — Cut Clips Quickly  
**Shortcut: C**  
Switch to the Razor Tool instantly and cut clips with a single click. Perfect for splitting long footage into smaller segments.  

---

## 2. Selection Tool  
**Shortcut: V**  
Quickly go back to the Selection Tool after cutting or adjusting clips. This is your default “safe zone” when editing.  

---

## 3. Ripple Delete  
**Shortcut: Shift + Delete**  
Removes a clip and automatically closes the gap between the remaining clips — a huge time saver.  

---

## 4. Add Edit at Playhead  
**Shortcut: Ctrl/Cmd + K**  
Cuts the clip exactly at the playhead position without switching tools. One of the most used shortcuts in editing.  

---

## 5. Extend Selected Edit to Playhead  
**Shortcut: E**  
Extends the selected edit point directly to the playhead, helping you fine-tune edit points quickly.  

---

## 6. Zoom In/Out on Timeline  
**Shortcut: + / - (Plus / Minus Keys)**  
Easily zoom into your timeline for precise editing or zoom out for an overview.  

---

## 7. Mark In/Out Points  
**Shortcut: I (In) / O (Out)**  
Set in and out points for clips or sequences to define exactly what section you want to use.  

---

## 8. Play/Pause Preview  
**Shortcut: Spacebar**  
The most intuitive shortcut — quickly start or stop playback of your sequence.  

---

## 9. Full Screen Program Monitor  
**Shortcut: Ctrl/Cmd + ~ (Tilde)**  
View your video in full screen for a better preview without distractions.  

---

## 10. Export Media  
**Shortcut: Ctrl/Cmd + M**  
Jump straight to the export settings window to render your project. Saves time when delivering final videos.  

---

# Final Thoughts  

Learning these shortcuts can save you hours of editing time each week.  
Start by memorizing 2–3 at a time and gradually add more to your workflow.  

Mastering shortcuts is one of the fastest ways to become more efficient and professional in **Premiere Pro**.
