## 1. Transitions

1. Place the **playhead between two layers** where the transition should occur.
2. Open the **Transitions** category in Odin Pro.
3. Hover over a transition and click **Apply**.
4. Adjust the **duration** by stretching the transition layer in the timeline.
5. Some transitions can be customized using **Essential Properties**.
6. Transitions automatically match the **composition resolution**.

## 2. Elements

1. Open the **Elements AE** section in Odin Pro.
2. **Double-click** an element to add it to the current composition.
3. Control the **in/out animation speed** by moving the **marker** in the timeline.
4. Adjust the overall **duration** by stretching the composition or its layer.
5. To customize an element:
   - Open its internal composition.
   - Use the **Control** layer for main settings.
   - Edit **text layers** directly to change titles.
6. To replace media:
   - Drop your video or image into the **placeholder composition**.
7. **Essential Properties** allow independent control of duplicated elements,  
   but editing text and media via Essential Properties is **not recommended**,  
   as it may break the structure.
