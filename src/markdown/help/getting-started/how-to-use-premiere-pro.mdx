## 1. Transitions

1. Place the **playhead exactly between two clips** on the timeline.
2. In Odin Pro, open the **Transitions** category.
3. To apply a transition:
   - **Double-click** the transition, or
   - **Drag and drop** it onto the cut on the timeline.
4. Use **Link Selection** in Premiere Pro to move the entire transition as a single layer.
5. Some transitions can be customized in the **Effect Controls** panel.
6. Use the **SFX** button if you want to apply a transition **without sound effects**.
7. Transitions automatically adapt to the **sequence resolution**.
8. For **shape transitions**, you can change their **colors** in the **Properties** panel.

## 2. Titles & MOGRT

1. Open the **MOGRT / Titles** section in Odin Pro.
2. Apply an element by:
   - **Double-clicking** it, or
   - **Dragging it** onto the timeline.
3. Customize **text and parameters**:
   - Via **Properties** inside Odin Pro, or
   - Via **Essential Graphics** (Premiere Pro versions below 25), or
   - Via **Effect Controls** when available.
4. To replace media:
   - Drop your media into the **Placeholder** field in **Properties**.
   - **Important:** replace media **only via Properties**.  
     Effect Controls do **not** support correct media replacement.
5. Change duration by **stretching the layer** on the timeline.
