https://odin-pro.com/account/subscription

---

## Devices & Seats

- **One seat** allows you to use Odin Pro on **up to 2 devices** owned by the **same user**.
- You can **increase or decrease the number of seats** by adding additional users to your license.
- Each added seat is intended for a **separate user**.

This allows teams and studios to legally use Odin Pro across multiple devices and users.
