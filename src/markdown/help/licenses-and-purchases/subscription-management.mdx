# Subscription Management

## Next Billing Date

When you purchase a subscription, you can view the **next billing date** in your account:

www.odin-pro.com/account/subscription

Navigate to:
**Order details → Period**

---

## Auto-Renewal

All subscriptions are subject to **automatic renewal**.

---

## Cancel Subscription or Change Payment Details

To cancel a subscription or update your payment details:

1. Go to:
   www.odin-pro.com/account/subscription
2. Click **View payment details**
3. You will be redirected to the **payment system login page**
4. Enter the **email address** used during purchase
5. Restore your password if needed  
   (you will receive a password recovery email)
6. After logging into your payment system account, you can:
   - Cancel the selected subscription
   - Update payment details for your subscription
