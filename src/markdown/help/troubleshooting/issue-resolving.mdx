# Issue Resolving

If you experience any issues while installing or using Odin Pro, please contact our support team.

---

## Contact Support

📧 **Email:**  
[help@odin-pro.com](mailto:help@odin-pro.com)

📝 **Contact Form:**  
https://odin-pro.com/contact

---

When contacting support, please include:

- Your Odin Pro account email
- The name and version of your software (Premiere Pro or After Effects)
- A short description of the issue
- Screenshots or screen recordings (if available)

This will help us resolve your issue faster.
