# Refunds & Returns

## Purchases made on odin-pro.com

**When can I get a refund?**

- No reason required if the purchase was made within 7 days.
- The product is not working as advertised or has a defect and the purchase was made within 30 days.

**How to ask for a refund**

To ask for a refund you can use the following [form](https://odin-pro.com/contact)

**When can you expect the refund**

It can take up to 30 business days to process & return the funds to your account. However, usually it's much sooner.
