Odin Pro Update
April 2026 Update

We’re excited to introduce a major update to Odin Pro, designed to improve usability, stability, and expand your creative workflow.

What’s new in version 1.3.0:

  • Unified Interface

    Subscription details and product information are now combined into a single, more intuitive and well-structured tab, making navigation easier and more efficient.

  • Improved Login & Download System

    All known issues related to logging into the extension and downloading project files have been resolved.
    ✅ If your account does not have an active subscription, a free demo pack will now install automatically.
    ✅ If your subscription is active, you can choose the destination folder where you'd like to install your pack.

  • New Content Added

    We've expanded the library with new creative elements, including titles, multi-screen layouts, and lower thirds.

Important Notice

After installing version 1.3.0 via Odin Pro Manager:
⚠️ You will be asked to log in to your account again
⚠️ You will need to download the pack again

Once the update is installed, please restart your Adobe Premiere Pro
or After Effects to ensure everything works correctly.


If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact our support team.
